Muse Gadget Installer

Flash the Muse Gadget SDK onto an ESP32 board from your browser. No ESP-IDF needed.

  2. From gadgets.muse.ai → Account → SDK tokens. It's written into the firmware right here in your browser. It isn't uploaded or saved anywhere.

  3. Plug in the board over USB and install Your browser can't talk to USB serial devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Serial access is blocked here. Open this page over https or localhost.

    Pick a board and paste a valid token first.

    If it can't connect, hold BOOT, tap RESET, let go of BOOT, and try again. Installing erases the board, including anything already on it (ESPHome, Arduino…) and any earlier pairing, so you'll set it up in the Muse app again afterwards.

  4. Set it up in the Muse app

    When the light breathes orange, open the Muse app, turn on Settings → Devices → Developer mode, then Add Device (the + icon). Pick MuseGadget-XXXXXX. When the light breathes blue, press the board's button to confirm.

Community project, not made by Meta. Builds come from muse-gadget-sdk using that repo's own board list. Use of your token is covered by the Gadget SDK Terms. Flashing custom firmware can brick boards. Proceed at your own risk.
